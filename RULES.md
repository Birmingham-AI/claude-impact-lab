# Rules and Safety

## Timing

- **No early starts.** Project work and first commits may begin at or after 9:30 AM CDT on August 28, 2026. Frameworks and libraries may preexist, but the project logic and artifact must be created during the Lab.
- **Hard submission deadline: 2:00 PM CDT.** See [SCHEDULE.md](SCHEDULE.md).

## Submission

- One submission per team.
- The submission must be in a public GitHub repository for review. A non-code artifact (blueprint, research artifact, plan, campaign, operating process) can use a public repository to hold documents, diagrams, data, and instructions.
- See [SUBMISSIONS.md](SUBMISSIONS.md) for the required contents and the submission form.

## Data

- Use public, licensed, or clearly synthetic data. Follow source terms, licenses, rate limits, and attribution requirements.
- Never use attendee data, private client records, confidential nonprofit data, health records, HMIS records, personally identifiable information, or other sensitive records.
- Every synthetic dataset must be clearly labeled as synthetic (an `is_synthetic` field or equivalent) and must not be presented as live or real.

## System interactions

- Do not write to live government or nonprofit systems.
- Do not submit tickets, referrals, applications, or service requests.
- Do not scrape or overload public services.

## Human oversight

- Keep a person responsible for consequential decisions.
- Show sources, uncertainty, and the point where a trained person must review or take over.
- Do not present synthetic availability, capacity, eligibility, consensus, or demand as real.

## Disqualification

Organizers may disqualify unsafe projects, including projects that violate the data rules, write to live systems, or misrepresent synthetic information as real.

## Eligibility gate

Before scoring (see [JUDGING.md](JUDGING.md)), every submission is checked against this rule set. A submission that fails the eligibility gate is not scored and is not eligible to become a finalist or winner.
