# Submissions

## Deadline

**2:00 PM CDT, Friday, August 28, 2026.** See [SCHEDULE.md](SCHEDULE.md). A submission warning goes out at 1:45 PM CDT.

## What to submit

Exactly **one submission per team**, made through the GitHub Issue Form at `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/project-submission.yml` in this repository ("New Issue" → "Project Submission").

Your submission must point to a **public GitHub repository** containing your project. A non-code artifact (service blueprint, research artifact, implementation plan, campaign, operating process) may use the public repository to hold documents, diagrams, data, and instructions instead of code.

## Required contents of your project repository

Use [templates/TEAM_README_TEMPLATE.md](templates/TEAM_README_TEMPLATE.md) as your starting README. It must include:

- Team name and team ID.
- Optional team member names.
- Challenge and primary user.
- Problem and repeated workflow.
- What the project does.
- Data and evidence sources.
- Architecture or approach, including how Claude was used to build and how Claude appears in the result, if applicable.
- Link to the working artifact or files.
- What works today.
- Known limitations and simulated elements.
- Named next step toward a pilot.
- A 60-second demo video, if the artifact cannot be reviewed through a working public link.

## Required contents of the submission form

The Issue Form collects: team ID, team name, challenge, primary user, public project repository URL, summary, data/evidence sources, how Claude was used, what works now, known limitations, and named next step. Optional fields: demo URL and participant names.

The form also requires you to acknowledge:

- Your project is submitted in a public repository.
- All synthetic data is clearly labeled as synthetic.
- No sensitive or personally identifiable data was used.
- Consequential decisions in your workflow are marked for human review.

## After submission

Submissions are checked against the [eligibility gate](RULES.md#eligibility-gate) before scoring. See [JUDGING.md](JUDGING.md) for the rubric, gallery walk, finalist selection, and final judging.
