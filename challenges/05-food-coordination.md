# Challenge 5: Coordinate Birmingham's Food Support Network

**Subtitle:** Coordinate food before it goes to waste by matching changing supply, demand, and service information.

- **Primary user:** Food coordinator, resident, or frontline staff member.
- **Repeated moment:** Where should this go?
- **Outcome:** Confirmed match.

## Problem

Birmingham has food banks, pantries, meal programs, schools, faith communities, donors, volunteers, and people working against food scarcity. The gap is often coordination. Supply, demand, hours, eligibility, transportation, capacity, and freshness change faster than information moves.

## Challenge

Build one shared workflow that answers: **Where is food or support available now, where is unmet need, what transfer or referral should happen next, and who will confirm it?**

## What a strong artifact does

- Models a small network using public information and synthetic inventory or demand.
- Matches supply or referrals to concrete needs.
- Accounts for location, timing, eligibility, storage, transportation, and freshness.
- Makes ownership and status visible.
- Preserves verified emergency links.

## What not to build

- Another static directory.
- Anything storing private client lists.
- Anything that rations food by algorithm.
- Anything presenting synthetic availability as live.
