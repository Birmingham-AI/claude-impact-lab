# Challenge 1: Choose Birmingham's Next Big Civic Project

**Subtitle:** Make the big bet legible: move from many worthy efforts to one shared project people can understand and join.

- **Primary user:** Civic convener.
- **Repeated moment:** Choose and mobilize.
- **Outcome:** Shared next action.

## Problem

Birmingham has many organizations, plans, and projects working toward a better region. The concern is that the community often favors incremental action and has difficulty rallying around a bold, catalytic project. The missing piece may not be another idea. It may be a credible way to compare ideas, build shared understanding, and turn interest into coordinated commitment.

## Challenge

Build a tool or experience that helps a civic convener answer: **What ambitious project should Birmingham rally around now, why this project, and what can each stakeholder do next?**

## What a strong artifact does

- Uses public evidence to compare or explain a small set of candidates.
- Shows affected people, partners, dependencies, tradeoffs, and first commitments.
- Gives stakeholders a way to join or own a next step.
- Distinguishes evidence from opinion.
- Avoids claiming consensus that has not been earned.

## What not to build

- A generic idea board.
- A popularity contest.
- A manifesto with no owner.
- An opaque score with no explanation.
