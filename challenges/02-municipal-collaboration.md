# Challenge 2: Make Regional Services Easier to Navigate

**Subtitle:** One region, one clear front door: make one fragmented public service understandable across municipal boundaries.

- **Primary user:** Resident or frontline staff member.
- **Repeated moment:** Who owns this?
- **Outcome:** Correct handoff.

## Problem

Greater Birmingham contains many municipalities and public agencies. Websites, terms, eligibility rules, boundaries, and service channels differ. This creates duplicated effort, unclear ownership, and gaps that residents or staff must navigate.

## Challenge

Choose one high-friction service and build a shared coordination layer across at least three jurisdictions. Answer: **Who is responsible, what is available here, where is the overlap or gap, and what is the next action?**

## What a strong artifact does

- Normalizes public information without pretending agencies agreed to a shared policy.
- Accepts a plain-language question, address, or synthetic case.
- Returns the responsible entity, evidence, and next step.
- Shows overlaps and gaps.
- Preserves authoritative links.
- Flags stale or conflicting information.

## What not to build

- A portal for every public service.
- A live ticket system.
- An automated legal-responsibility determination.
