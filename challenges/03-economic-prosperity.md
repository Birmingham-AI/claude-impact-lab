# Challenge 3: Make Economic Opportunity Easier to Reach

**Subtitle:** Turn opportunity into a reachable next step for a resident or local business.

- **Primary user:** Worker, young person, navigator, or founder.
- **Repeated moment:** What can I do next?
- **Outcome:** Credible pathway.

## Problem

Birmingham has jobs, training, business support, funding, and civic initiatives, but opportunity is not equally reachable. Programs can be fragmented, hidden behind requirements, or disconnected from transportation, time, credentials, and eligibility.

## Challenge

Choose one pathway and answer: **Given this synthetic person or business today, what is the next credible opportunity, what barriers stand in the way, and what can happen in the next seven days?**

## What a strong artifact does

- Uses public opportunities and synthetic profiles.
- Shows two or three realistic options, requirements, action sequence, and handoff owner.
- Explains practical barriers.
- Lets a navigator see recurring gaps without exposing personal information.

## What not to build

- A generic directory.
- An opaque opportunity score.
- A prediction of success.
- A guarantee of employment, funding, or eligibility.
